Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 25, 2026

Meet Me Halfway is a fully client-side web app. There is no proprietary backend server. This page describes exactly what data leaves your device, where it goes, and how long it lives.

What we collect

  • Live location — while a session is active, your device shares its latitude, longitude, GPS accuracy, and a timestamp with the other participants in the same session.
  • Anonymous identifier — a random ID generated by Firebase Anonymous Auth. It is not linked to your name, email, phone number, or any account. It exists only to scope your writes inside the session.
  • Optional display name — if you type a name into the session badge, it is stored alongside your location for the duration of the session. It is visible only to other participants in that session.

We do not collect: email, phone number, contacts, photos, microphone audio, device identifiers, advertising IDs, or any persistent profile.

Where it goes

  • Firebase Realtime Database (Google Cloud) — stores your location reading at /sessions/{code}/participants/{uid}. Access is restricted by Firebase security rules to authenticated participants of the same session.
  • Mapbox — receives a routing request (origin and destination coordinates) when computing driving or walking routes. Subject to Mapbox's privacy policy.
  • Google Places API (optional) — if venue search is enabled by the operator, the midpoint coordinates are sent to Google to fetch nearby places. Subject to Google's privacy policy.
  • Sentry (optional) — if error tracking is enabled by the operator, JavaScript exceptions are reported. No location data is ever attached. A failure report may include the first two characters of the session code, plus whether the browser was online and whether it was an in-app browser — enough to correlate reports, not enough to identify you or locate the session.

How long it stays

Each session expires automatically 24 hours after creation and all participant data is deleted. Your location is overwritten with each new reading (no historical track is retained). When you close the tab, a best-effort cleanup removes your record immediately.

Sharing

Your live location is visible only to the people you invite via the session link. The 6-character session code is the only access credential — share it carefully. Anyone with the link can join the session up to a maximum of 5 participants.

Your choices

  • Revoke location permission in your browser at any time. The app will stop sharing immediately.
  • Close the tab or browser to end your participation. Your record is removed.
  • Don't share the session link with people you don't want to share your location with.

Children

Meet Me Halfway is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to this policy

We will update the “Last updated” date above when this policy changes. Material changes will be announced on the project's GitHub repository.
